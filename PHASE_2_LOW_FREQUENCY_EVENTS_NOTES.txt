PHASE 2 — LOW FREQUENCY EVENT BALANCE

Separate build.

New target:
- 3 to 5 routine/discretionary lifecycle popups per season.
- Each season receives a stable target of 3, 4 or 5.

Routine events:
- minimum roughly 6–8 weeks apart
- exact event cooldown ~330 days
- topic/family cooldown ~240 days
- ordinary topic can appear only once per season
- recent sources/topics are heavily deprioritised

Examples:
- Women's Development should not appear twice in one normal season.
- Grassroots funding should not keep recycling.
- Media outlets should rotate more naturally.

NOT counted in the 3–5 limit:
- timed commitments coming due
- forced crises
- major events explicitly marked as such

Goal:
Popups should feel significant rather than becoming the core way time progression is interrupted.
