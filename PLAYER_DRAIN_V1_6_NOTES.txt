Player Drain Index V1.6

Adds two hidden measures:
- Current Drain: present impact of England-relevant players currently overseas.
- Structural Drain: slower-moving persistent pressure caused by sustained player loss.

Player weighting uses:
- age / career stage
- England status
- caps
- years already abroad
- generated future-player status

Drain contribution is split into:
- young
- prime
- senior
- veteran
- generated-player contribution

Normal movement remains hidden.
Only sustained serious structural drain can create a Professional Game Concern RFU event.
