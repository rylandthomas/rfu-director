RFU DIRECTOR — LIVE MATCH STATS FIX

Fixed a future-data leak in the international match screen.

Previously:
- scoreboard used events up to the current minute;
- England tries displayed the FINAL simulated match try total;
- possession/territory displayed one precomputed future block value.

Now:
- score is event-derived up to the displayed minute;
- tries are counted only from try events that have occurred by that minute;
- possession is averaged only from completed simulation blocks up to that minute;
- territory is averaged only from completed simulation blocks up to that minute.

Example:
At 18' and England leading 7-3, England Tries will show 1 unless a second try has
actually occurred before 18'.
