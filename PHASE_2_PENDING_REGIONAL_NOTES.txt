PHASE 2 — PENDING QUESTIONS + REGIONAL RUGBY

Separate build. Previous Phase 2 builds remain unchanged.

1) PENDING / MINIMISABLE QUESTIONS
- Media and stakeholder questions now include "Review RFU data".
- This minimises the question into a persistent bottom bar.
- You can navigate the game normally while the question is pending.
- +1 Day, +1 Week and Next Event are locked until you answer.
- "Return to question" reopens the pending interview.
- The original question/source/context is preserved.

2) ENGLISH RUGBY -> REGIONAL RUGBY
English Rugby now includes 8 management regions:
- North East
- North West
- Yorkshire
- Midlands
- East
- London
- South East
- South West

Each tracks:
- registered players
- youth players
- active clubs
- volunteers
- facilities
- referees
- annual RFU investment
- community confidence
- participation trend

3) REGIONAL SIMULATION
- Regions update periodically.
- Grassroots / Schools / Referee programmes affect regional outcomes.
- Community confidence and RFU investment affect growth.
- National registered/youth totals are synced from the regions.

4) REGIONAL MEDIA QUESTIONS
Regional media events now use actual regional data.
Example:
"South West rugby growth attracts local media attention"
The question can mention growth, volunteers, facilities and annual RFU investment.

TEST
- Trigger a media/stakeholder question.
- Click Review RFU data.
- Confirm the question minimises.
- Navigate to English Rugby.
- Confirm all 8 regional cards are visible.
- Try +1 Week / +1 Day: they should be disabled while question is pending.
- Return to question and answer it.
- Confirm time controls unlock.
- When a regional media question appears, inspect the named region before answering.
