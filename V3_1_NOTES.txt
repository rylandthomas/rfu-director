V3.1 calibration update

Why:
The first V3 audit still made RFU relevance too dependent on formal playing-budget cuts.

Changes:
- retention risk now also responds to commercial strain, owner commitment and normal contract cycles;
- contract reviews exist in the stress test even at otherwise stable clubs;
- overseas-move probability is influenced by club retention pressure AND England selection policy;
- Domestic-only policy suppresses overseas moves but creates the highest individual eligibility consequence when a move happens;
- Open selection increases overseas movement but creates no individual eligibility issue;
- Open/service policies add systemic player-drain pressure to the English club game;
- RFU retention meetings occur only when a real eligibility consequence exists;
- pathway/governance/continuity routes remain independent;
- event frequency remains moderated so Newcastle is vulnerable rather than permanently chaotic.

Transfer cycles are accelerated in the audit only. The live game still has no manufactured named-player transfers yet.
