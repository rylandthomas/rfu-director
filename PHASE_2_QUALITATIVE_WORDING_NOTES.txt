PHASE 2 — QUALITATIVE EVENT WORDING

Event/interview questions no longer expose internal relationship or programme scores such as 82/100.

Examples:
82 relationship -> "very strong"
73 relationship -> "strong"
55 relationship -> "mixed but stable"
38 relationship -> "poor"

Programme ratings are similarly translated into natural language such as:
"performing strongly", "in good health", "showing mixed progress", or "causing growing concern".

Dashboard/statistical screens are unchanged; this applies to contextual question/event prose.
