RFU Director V1.9.1 — Professional Rugby Actionability

Professional Rugby is no longer observation-only.

Added RFU Policy tab:
- Central club distributions: constrained/current/enhanced
- Professional academy & pathway support
- England player access policy
- Player welfare/rest policy
- Partnership negotiating stance
- Formal PGP review with cooldown
- Recent RFU professional-game decision history

Design principle:
Professional clubs remain autonomous. The RFU controls the things an RFU Director should:
funding frameworks, access, welfare, pathway obligations, governance and the Professional Game Partnership.

Policy changes:
- update real annual RFU expenditure
- influence club relationships/stability gradually
- influence player relationships/welfare
- influence academy development
- persist in the career save
- avoid repeat-click exploits by applying most consequences over time

The old raw 'Club Health' tab has been removed from the primary navigation and replaced by RFU Policy.
