PHASE 2 — HUMAN REACTION UPDATE

Separate build.

Changed:
- Removed engine-like feedback language from response reactions.
- Stakeholder/media feedback is now written from the perspective of the group reacting.
- Reactions vary by:
  * topic
  * hidden answer quality
  * response tone
  * whether a public commitment was made

Topic-specific reaction sets now cover:
- Community Rugby
- England performance
- Club/RFU relationship
- Player welfare
- Schools/youth
- Academy/pathway access
- Commercial reputation
- Governance/media scrutiny

The hidden response interpreter still runs, but the player is never told that an answer was
"strong", "adequate", "weak" or "failed" by the interface. That assessment only selects the
natural-language reaction shown.
