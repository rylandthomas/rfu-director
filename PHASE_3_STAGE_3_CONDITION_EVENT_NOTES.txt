PHASE 3 STAGE 3 — CONDITION-LED EVENT ENGINE

NEW EVENT FLOW
Healthy / improving -> occasional informational news article
Stable -> usually silence
Early decline -> warning article
Poor / critical -> intervention event requiring response
Successful intervention -> later positive outcome article
Poor intervention -> later escalation article

PROGRAMME THRESHOLDS
75+ healthy
60-74 watch
45-59 poor
below 45 critical

HEALTHY SYSTEMS
Generic questions are suppressed for:
- Grassroots
- Referees
- Schools
- Women's Rugby
- Academy Pathway
when programme health is 60+.

CONSEQUENCE BALANCE
Good intervention:
- small positive relationship effect (+1 / +2)
- small programme recovery
Bad intervention:
- heavy relationship penalty (-5 / -8)
- programme deterioration
- later negative follow-up article

GENERIC EVENTS
Routine generic media questions are now extremely rare:
- maximum 2 per season
- at least 14 weeks apart
- very low random chance

Most popups should now be articles, not questions.
