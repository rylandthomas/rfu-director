PHASE 2 — FREE-TEXT FINANCIAL COMMITMENTS

Separate build.

Implemented:
- Explicit free-text spending commitments now affect actual RFU cash.
- Examples recognised:
  "We will invest £1m in referee infrastructure."
  "We commit £750k to community clubs."
  "We will spend £2 million on schools rugby."

Not treated as immediate commitments:
  "We have already invested £1m..."
  "We could invest £1m..."
  "We should consider £1m..."
  "We will review whether £1m is needed..."

Money is routed by subject:
- referees -> Referee Development + delayed referee-availability effect
- grassroots -> Community/Grassroots + participation consequence
- schools/youth -> Schools programme + youth consequence
- academies/pathway -> Academy/U20 consequence
- coaching -> Coach Development
- facilities -> delayed facility/community consequence
- professional clubs -> club relationship/support
- player welfare -> player relationship/preparation

Referee questions now have a dedicated response profile.
A response such as:
  "We will commit £1m to grassroots referee infrastructure."
should:
- be recognised as directly relevant
- deduct £1m immediately
- record the finance transaction
- improve referee/community outlook
- schedule a later referee-availability consequence
- produce a natural referee-specific reaction
