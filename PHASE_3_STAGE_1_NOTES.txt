RFU DIRECTOR — PHASE 3 / STAGE 1
ENGLISH PROFESSIONAL RUGBY ECOSYSTEM

Built from the frozen Phase 2 checkpoint. Phase 2 is untouched.

Stage 1 introduces persistent Premiership club organisations.

Each club now has:
- squad strength
- academy quality
- facilities
- financial health
- cash/debt
- supporter strength
- RFU relationship
- organisational stability
- England-player count
- attendance
- form and persistent league record

A Premiership simulation layer has also been added:
- results/round progression
- W/L/table points
- points difference
- club form
- monthly financial/stability movement
- slow academy development when clubs are healthy

Professional Rugby now exposes:
- Premiership table
- most vulnerable club
- strongest academy
- England player concentration
- individual club-health cards
