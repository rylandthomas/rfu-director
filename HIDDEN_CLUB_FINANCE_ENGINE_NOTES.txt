RFU DIRECTOR — HIDDEN CLUB FINANCE ENGINE

Implemented directly into the professional-game simulation.

Hidden per-club drivers:
- stadium capacity
- underlying attendance demand
- actual home-match attendance
- average ticket yield
- match-day ancillary spend
- annual commercial strength
- RFU / central distribution income
- owner support
- player wage bill
- staff / operating costs
- academy costs
- debt service
- other operating costs

How it runs:
- every simulated Premiership home game quietly calculates attendance and match-day income;
- attendance responds to club support, form, league position, opponent draw, season importance and controlled variation;
- attendance is capped by stadium capacity;
- every month, hidden match-day income is combined with commercial, central and owner income;
- wages, staff/operations, academy spending, debt service and other costs are deducted;
- the monthly operating result changes cash, debt, financial health and stability;
- prolonged poor results can create borrowing/debt pressure and pathway deterioration;
- strong sustainable finances can modestly support academy development.

Professional Game Stage 2 is now linked to this output:
club-support events no longer rely on a purely seeded finance score; the visible financial-health state is synchronised from the hidden operating model.

Attendance is deliberately NOT shown anywhere in the normal Premiership or Championship UI.
It can still influence news/narratives later when a trend becomes strategically relevant.
