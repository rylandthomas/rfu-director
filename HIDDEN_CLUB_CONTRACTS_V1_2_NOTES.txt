Hidden Club Contracts V1.2

Changes from V1.1:
- fixed repeated-renewal cycle bug;
- realistic new-deal lengths by age;
- formal review blocked until final 12 months of the NEW deal;
- renewal outcome is probabilistic rather than deterministic;
- player form, fitness, age, status and caps influence market interest;
- domestic/overseas interest is dynamic;
- non-renewal now records a reason:
  club affordability
  club chooses release
  player rejects terms
  security gap
  overseas interest
  domestic interest
  age profile
  terms not agreed
- no actual transfers yet;
- club contract data remains hidden from RFU UI.
