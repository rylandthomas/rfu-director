PHASE 2 — COMMITMENT DECISION SYSTEM

Separate build.

Timed commitment follow-ups are no longer free-text interviews.

When a commitment comes due, the popup now shows:
- original wording
- promised timing
- recorded amount (if any)
- concrete management options

Options:
- Honour the promised amount
- Increase the commitment
- Reduce the commitment
- Delay by 1 month
- Delay by 3 months
- Delay by 6 months
- Withdraw commitment

Effects:
- Honour/increase/reduce can deduct real RFU cash
- Relevant programme and delayed consequences are applied
- Delay creates a new deadline and stakeholder penalties
- Withdraw avoids immediate expenditure but causes a larger trust/reputation hit
- Decisions are written into Inbox & Media

Referee example:
A previously promised £500k referee commitment will now return as a proper decision screen,
not another free-text question.
