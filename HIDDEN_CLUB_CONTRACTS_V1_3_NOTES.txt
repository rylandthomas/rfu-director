Hidden Club Contracts V1.3

Starting player cohort
- Updated to the current 2026/27 England enhanced EPS cohort.
- Real-world ages, clubs and recent England caps are used as the simulation baseline where available.
- In-game player names remain deliberately altered.

Career lifecycle
- Players age each season.
- Retirement becomes increasingly likely from the early 30s.
- Key England players can extend careers somewhat longer.
- Retired players leave the active England pool.
- A new generated England-relevant prospect enters to keep the long-term talent pool alive.

Hidden contract movement
- Failed club renewals are now resolved in the hidden world.
- Possible outcomes: another English club, overseas club, one-year bridge deal, or retirement.
- England selection policy changes the attractiveness/consequence of an overseas move.
- Club contract figures remain hidden from RFU UI.

RFU relevance
- Domestic moves stay silent.
- Overseas moves that preserve England eligibility stay silent.
- Overseas moves that make a significant England player ineligible create an RFU-facing eligibility event.
- Raw club salary, offer and affordability data are never shown to the RFU Director.
