PHASE 3 STAGE 3 — CONDITION SEVERITY REFINEMENT

Event severity now uses:
- programme score
- direction of travel
- current annual RFU funding

Examples:
60 and stable/improving -> silence
60 and declining -> warning article only
50-59 with meaningful funding -> usually warning, not intervention
45-49 -> intervention only if deterioration is genuine
below 45 -> critical intervention territory

Existing RFU investment is acknowledged in article/intervention wording.

Referee example:
Referee Development 60/100 with £3m annual funding will no longer trigger
"Referee shortage requires RFU intervention" solely because the score is 60.

The expected behaviour is silence or a softer monitoring article unless the trend worsens.
