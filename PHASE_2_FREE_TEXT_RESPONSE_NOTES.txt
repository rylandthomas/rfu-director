PHASE 2 FREE-TEXT RESPONSE SYSTEM

- Media and stakeholder conversations now use tone + free text.
- Operational/financial decisions keep buttons.
- Hidden parser checks relevance, coverage, action, evasiveness, dismissiveness, length and keyword stuffing.
- Grades: Strong / Adequate / Weak / Failed.
- Off-topic answers are negative even with a polite tone.
- Tone is a second layer: Positive, Neutral, Diplomatic, Firm, Abrupt, Defensive.
- Public commitments can be remembered and revisited later.
- Immediate article/reaction explains whether the answer landed, without revealing numeric buffs/debuffs.
