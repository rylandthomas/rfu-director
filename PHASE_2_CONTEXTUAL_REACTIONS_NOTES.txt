PHASE 2 — CONTEXTUAL REACTION IMPROVEMENT

Separate build.

Key improvement:
- Reaction/article text now pulls concepts directly from the player's response.
- Recognised concepts include:
  coaching, investment, grassroots, facilities, schools, youth, academies,
  players, clubs, welfare, finances, private-business/remit arguments,
  supporters, media, reviews and future action.

Example:
Player:
"Investment in facilities and grassroots rugby has helped."

The reaction can now say:
"The RFU attributed England’s progress to continued investment across the game,
improved facilities and the grassroots and community game."

instead of:
"The response touched on the issue but lacked detail."

Brief-but-relevant answers are treated as concise, not automatically poor or robotic.

The hidden interpreter still scores relevance and stakeholder effects underneath,
but the player-facing reaction is now assembled around what the player actually said.
