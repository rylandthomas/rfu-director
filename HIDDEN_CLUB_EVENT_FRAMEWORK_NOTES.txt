Hidden club-event framework

Club-level events are now simulated internally and do not automatically appear to the RFU Director.

Hidden event types:
- owner commitment reduction
- playing-budget cut
- academy/pathway cut
- commercial gain
- commercial loss
- owner investment
- debt restructuring
- sporting momentum

Persistent hidden modifiers:
owner support, commercial strength, wage budget, player retention, recruitment, academy investment, operating costs.

Events have durations, stack, expire, feed into the accounts-based finance engine, and slowly influence squad/academy strength.

RFU relevance filter:
Potential England-retention pressure is recorded as a hidden signal only. It does NOT create an inbox story.
A future player contract/transfer consequence must actually affect England eligibility before the RFU is notified.

Developer audit:
stress_test_hidden_club_events.js is included only to inspect hidden event frequency.
