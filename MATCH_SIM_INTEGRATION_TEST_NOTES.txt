RFU DIRECTOR — FULL-SCREEN INTERNATIONAL MATCH FLOW

Revision 2.

FLOW
1. Advance to the next England international.
2. The normal RFU dashboard is replaced by a dedicated Match Day intro screen.
3. Intro lightly shows:
   - fixture / competition
   - home or away
   - England ranking
   - opponent ranking
   - England recent form
   - recent H2H from recorded save results
   - light opponent form indicator
4. Nothing simulates until Kick Off is clicked.
5. Kick Off opens the dedicated full-screen match simulation.
6. The 80-minute match plays over about 120 real seconds.
7. No manager controls, pitch or tactical decisions.
8. At FT, Continue returns to the RFU Director dashboard.

This uses a separate save key so testing does not overwrite the previous integration save.
