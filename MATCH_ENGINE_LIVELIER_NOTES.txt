RFU DIRECTOR — LIVELIER INTERNATIONAL MATCH ENGINE

Added non-scoring rugby events to the match feed while preserving the existing score engine.

New visible events:
- missed conversion
- knock-on
- scrum awarded
- scrum won
- kick to touch
- pressure relieved
- attacking kick to corner
- lineout awarded
- lineout won
- lineout stolen
- turnover won
- clearance kick

Events are state-aware:
- knock-ons consider fatigue, territory and attacking quality;
- scrum outcomes consider scrum ratings;
- lineout outcomes consider lineout ratings;
- pressure-relief kicking is more likely in poor field position;
- attacking lineouts are more likely in advanced territory;
- turnovers are more likely during longer phase sequences.

No player names are used yet.
Once player data is added, these same event hooks can become:
'Smith knocks on', 'Itoje steals the lineout', etc.
