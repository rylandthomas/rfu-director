PHASE 3 — STAGE 3: PROFESSIONAL PYRAMID MOVEMENT

Implemented first Stage 3 layer:

- Champ Rugby champion is reviewed at season end.
- Promotion operating standard checks:
  * Facilities >= 65
  * Financial health >= 60
  * Organisational stability >= 58
- Champion must meet the standard; the game does not automatically skip to second place.
- Eligible champion is promoted.
- Bottom PREM club is relegated.
- Club ecosystem records move with the club rather than being recreated.
- Promotion/relegation affects supporters, stability, finances and attendance.
- Blocked promotion produces no relegation.
- Professional pyramid history is recorded.
- Championship cards explain which standards a failing club still needs.

This is the foundation of Stage 3. RFU intervention/funding cases around clubs approaching
promotion standards are the next layer after this movement logic is tested.
