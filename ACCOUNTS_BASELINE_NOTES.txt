Accounts-based club finance calibration

Published anchor:
2023/24 turnover / reported result baseline for the ten Premiership clubs.
Newcastle uses the latest figure available in the comparison source.

Game-start projection:
The 2023/24 anchor is projected to the 2026 game start using modest model assumptions:
- turnover +4% per year
- costs +2.5% per year

These are simulation assumptions, not claimed audited 2026 club figures.

Attendance:
2023/24 attendance is used only to calibrate hidden matchday demand.
Showpiece fixtures that took Harlequins/Saracens outside their normal home stadium are normalised for ordinary weekly demand; their commercial value remains captured in the published turnover baseline.

The club business model starts from accounts turnover and implied costs.
Attendance, results, owner backing, debt, costs and commercial drift then move clubs away from that baseline.
