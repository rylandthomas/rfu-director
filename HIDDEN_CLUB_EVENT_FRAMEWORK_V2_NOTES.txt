V2 hidden club event framework

Changes:
- hidden events now depend on actual club pressure state;
- clubs choose between several financial/organisational responses;
- repeated event types have long cooldowns;
- budget cuts cannot simply fire every few months forever;
- persistent pressure escalates the chance/severity of management action;
- recovery reduces escalation;
- academy cuts can now occur under deeper pressure;
- owner investment, debt restructuring, recruitment freezes and commercial restructuring compete with budget cuts as responses;
- recovery can trigger academy reinvestment;
- commercial events remain sparse;
- hidden events remain invisible to the RFU unless a later England consequence makes them relevant.

Run stress_test_hidden_club_events_v2.js for the updated audit.
