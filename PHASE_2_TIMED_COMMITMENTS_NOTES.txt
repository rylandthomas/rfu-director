PHASE 2 — TIMED COMMITMENTS

Separate build.

Implemented:
- The interpreter now understands timing phrases such as:
  * end of the season
  * after the Six Nations
  * after the autumn internationals
  * after the World Cup
  * next month
  * in X weeks
  * in X months

Example:
"We will review the problem at the end of the season and re-evaluate."

Now becomes:
- a stored Premiership funding commitment
- due at season end
- routine repeats of the same funding question are suppressed until then
- once the date arrives, the game actively raises:
  "Premiership clubs await promised RFU funding review"

If the player delays again:
- a new six-week deadline is created
- stakeholder/media penalties apply

If the player honours/closes the review:
- the commitment is marked complete

Timed commitments are handled separately from generic six-month promise checks.
