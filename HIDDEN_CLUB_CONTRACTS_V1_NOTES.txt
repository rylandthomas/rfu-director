Hidden club contracts V1

This is deliberately NOT an RFU-facing contract system.

Every England-relevant player now has hidden club employment data:
- current club
- contract expiry
- hidden salary band / approximate internal salary
- club retention priority
- club affordability
- renewal intent
- domestic interest
- overseas interest
- English service years
- stable hidden player decision profile:
  money, England ambition, loyalty, security, career ambition and overseas interest

Monthly contract reviews:
- begin inside the final 12 months;
- can result in early renewal;
- can create hidden retention-pressure cases;
- can fail at expiry.

V1 intentionally DOES NOT move a player after failed renewal.
It leaves an unresolved hidden contract case for the next phase:
hidden player movement / domestic and overseas offers.

No club salary, club offer, affordability score or contract detail is added to the RFU UI.
